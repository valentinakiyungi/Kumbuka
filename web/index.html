    Get Kumbuka

    Install as an app

    Opens in its own window with a Kumbuka icon. Works on computers and phones; nothing else to set up.

    Kumbuka is installed. Open it from your apps, dock or home screen.

      Run it on your own computer

      Your chats and memories stay on your machine, and Kumbuka uses your own free Gemini key. Needs Python 3.10 or newer and pipx.

      pipx install git+https://github.com/valentinakiyungi/Kumbuka.git
      kumbuka

      The first run asks for a key from Google AI Studio, then opens Kumbuka in your browser. Docker and source installs are on GitHub.
